The work
behind the numbers.

IEEE · 31 Oct 2023 · Peer-reviewed

Radio Map Estimation with
Deep Dual Path Autoencoders & Skip Connection Learning

Predicting radio power at every location and frequency in a 2D area from sparse measurements — relevant to fingerprint localization, user-cell association, MIMO power control, and UAV path planning. The paper introduces the Dual Path Autoencoder (separating environmental and signal information) and Skip Connection Autoencoders (increasing encoder-decoder information flow), and benchmarks them against state-of-the-art CNN autoencoders across sampling rates and architecture sizes.
